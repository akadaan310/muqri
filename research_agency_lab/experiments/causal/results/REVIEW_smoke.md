### What the boundary data support

**Observed.**
- For each unit, the middle level of the first transform was applied to the Viterbi span, and to the same span shifted by ±20 ms (half a 40 ms frame).
- Compared with the edit at the span, the shifted edits changed Muqri metrics by these maxima:
  - ز: 4.17 nats (makhraj:ذ, +20 ms)
  - ص: 5.69 nats (makhraj:ز, −20 ms)
  - ghunnah: 7.16 nats (makhraj:م, −20 ms)
- Many changes exceed the 1-nat floor, and for ز and ص many exceed same-letter natural variation (the tables above).
- **The effect is asymmetric and differs by unit.**
  - ghunnah: at −20 ms the nasal edit's effect on every Muqri metric nearly vanishes (e.g. identity 44.03 against 40.35 at the span; baseline 44.20). At +20 ms it reproduces the span result (40.25). Yet the physical nasal-ratio change is essentially the same in all three placements: −20.89, −20.69 and −20.51 dB.
  - ص: the **target** (tafkhim margin) is stable under both shifts (|Δ| 0.09), while several non-target metrics move by 2–6 nats.
  - ز: the target (hams/jahr) moves 1.08 nats at +20 ms and 0.42 at −20 ms.
- The physics of the ±20 ms records is measured on the shifted span, against the baseline's unshifted span, so their physics deltas mix the edit with different audio.

**Interpretation.**
- The data support this: *the Muqri outcome of an edit is sensitive, at the 20 ms scale, to where the edit is placed*, often more than to the edit's strength (for ghunnah, a −20 ms placement cancels an edit that physically measures the same).
- So conclusions drawn from edits placed at 40 ms Viterbi boundaries carry a placement uncertainty of this size.
- The data do **not** show that the Viterbi boundaries are wrong by any amount: no ground-truth boundaries exist in this run. Nor do they separate *boundary error* from *which 20 ms of audio is edited*: a shifted edit necessarily changes different samples.
- The statement "40 ms Viterbi boundaries are a real limit on precision" is supported only in that weaker form: placement at the frame scale materially changes the measured Muqri response. Its stronger form (the boundaries are inaccurate) is not tested.

## 8. Code-path inspection (harness.py at 48c4c6e; line numbers are that file's)

- **Where Muqri is invoked.**
  - `Harness.analyze` (l.156–157) calls `self.eng.analyze(wave, [(s, a)], makhraj=True)["measurements"]` on the **whole ayah**.
  - It is used for the baseline (l.209), the determinism re-run (l.226), and every variant (l.219, inside `run`).
- **Is the production scoring path unchanged?**
  - `app/` is untouched (no diff since the Observatory commits).
  - The harness calls the same `Engine.analyze` as `/sessions`, but with `makhraj=True`. That flag adds the neighbour margins; per its implementation, it never changes identity or characteristic verdicts.
  - Session scoring (`app/sessions.score`) is **not** used; the harness reads the raw measurements.
- **How comparisons are paired.** Each variant's Muqri view is differenced against the **same** untouched-baseline analysis `m0`/`mb` of the same ayah, metric by metric (the unit's letters are addressed by text-position IDs such as `70:5:L6`).
- **How controls are generated.** `run(...)` calls `transforms.apply`, which returns the edited ayah and the new span end:
  - determinism: a second `analyze` on the untouched wave
  - `noop_splice`: the original samples spliced back
  - `noop_world`: WORLD analysis and resynthesis of span ±0.3 s
  - `noop_pv`: the phase vocoder at k = 1.0
  - boundary: the same middle-level edit on span ±0.02 s (l.234)
  - same-letter / neighbour: `perturb.splice` of a donor span from another ayah
  - The WORLD mask is a **hard** 0/1 mask over the span frames (transforms.py l.39), with no taper.
- **How the floors are applied.**
  - `schema.noise_floors` takes, per metric, the maximum |Δ| over the unit's determinism and noop_splice records, plus the family's own no-op (noop_world for voicing, formant, F0 and nasal; noop_pv for duration). This is raised to a minimum: `MIN_NOISE` = 1.0 nat, 0.041 s, 0.25 counts, 0.05 fraction, 15 Hz, 1 dB, 0.02 (ratio).
  - For physics the duration minimum is 0.005 s (harness l.245–253).
  - `schema.classify` then applies strict |Δ| > floor.
- **Manually chosen thresholds.** Yes:
  - the `MIN_NOISE` minima
  - `PARTIAL` when measured/predicted falls outside [0.5, 2]
  - the ±0.02 s boundary shift and the ±0.3 s WORLD context
  - the parameter levels
  - Praat settings: pitch 60–500 Hz, 5 formants at 5.5 kHz, nasal bands 150–400 and 750–1100 Hz
  - the review's own selectivity rule (section 4)

  The margin floors are raised by the no-ops, but in practice they sat at the 1-nat minimum for most heads (for example `noise_floor_muqri` = 1.000 in the boundary tables).
- **Could it compare differently segmented audio?** Yes, by construction:
  - Muqri re-aligns every edited ayah independently, so a letter ID's frames can move between baseline and variant. This is visible in `ز duration`, where the physics span changed and Muqri's letter duration did not.
  - Physics is measured on a span taken from the edit (`[t0, t0 + len(edited)/SR]`), not from Muqri's new alignment.
  - Duration edits change the ayah's length after the span.
  - Boundary records measure physics on the shifted span, against the baseline's unshifted span.
- **Are results deterministic?**
  - The determinism control gives Δ = 0 on every Muqri metric for all 3 units (stored).
  - The transforms were run once, so their run-to-run determinism was not tested by repetition (WORLD harvest and the librosa phase vocoder contain no random step).
  - Octave and Julia were run once.

## 10. Review conclusion

### OBSERVED
- **The instrument is sound at its base.** The engine is deterministic on these inputs (Δ = 0 on re-run). The pass-through splice and the phase vocoder at ×1 move no Muqri metric. WORLD resynthesis alone moves some makhraj margins by up to 2.2 nats and flips one nearby letter's tafkhim verdict (70:5:L12).
- **Physics.** Each transform changed its intended physical quantity as requested, measured independently: duration exactly; F0 within about 1 Hz; F2 +138 / +297 / +470 Hz against predicted +120 / +270 / +463; nasal ratio −10.5 / −20.7 / −30.4 dB. The exception is partial devoicing (60 % and 30 %), which Praat does not register.
- **Muqri's target responded, dose-dependently, in three cases:** ghunnah counts under stretching (−0.89, +0.85, +1.73), tafkhim under formant warp (−2.14, −6.42 at a = 0.8, 0.7), and ghonna under nasal attenuation (−1.59, −4.79 at −12, −18 dB).
